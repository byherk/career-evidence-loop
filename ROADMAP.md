# Roadmap

Career Evidence Loop is currently an exploratory research project.

The roadmap is intentionally provisional and should change as the research becomes clearer.

## Phase 0 — Problem framing and research mapping

Goals:

* define the problem precisely;
* map relevant research traditions;
* identify existing systems and adjacent work;
* clarify terminology;
* document major assumptions;
* identify scientific and ethical risks;
* invite interdisciplinary critique.

Current phase

## Phase 1 — Evidence model

Goals:

* define evidence types;
* distinguish observation, inference, and hypothesis;
* represent provenance;
* represent uncertainty;
* represent contradictory evidence;
* represent missing / not-yet-observed evidence;
* explore expert-feedback reliability.

## Phase 2 — Learner and career hypothesis model

Goals:

* define how learner capabilities may be represented over time;
* distinguish interest, demonstrated ability, learning velocity, motivation, and career fit;
* define how career hypotheses are created;
* define how hypotheses become stronger or weaker;
* identify exploration bias and feedback loops;
* connect learner evidence with occupational frameworks.

## Phase 3 — Conceptual prototype

Goals:

* build the smallest testable implementation;
* focus on a small number of career families;
* use synthetic or carefully governed data;
* test evidence provenance and hypothesis updating;
* evaluate explainability and uncertainty communication.

## Phase 4 — Human-in-the-loop pilot

Goals:

* involve educators and domain experts;
* test structured expert feedback;
* study disagreement between evaluators;
* examine learner reactions to recommendations and explanations;
* test whether proposed career experiments generate useful evidence.

## Phase 5 — Longitudinal evaluation

Long-term questions:

* Does the system improve learning decisions?
* Does it improve career exploration?
* Does it preserve learner agency?
* Does it reduce or amplify bias?
* Are its recommendations better calibrated over time?
* Do learners discover meaningful possibilities they otherwise might not have explored?

No transition between phases should be assumed automatic.

Evidence should determine what comes next.
