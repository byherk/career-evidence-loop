# Glossary

This glossary defines working terminology for the project.

Definitions are provisional and may change as the research develops.

## Observation

Something directly recorded or reported.

Example:

> The learner completed the task without assistance.

## Evidence

An observation or artifact that may support or challenge a claim.

Evidence should have provenance and context.

## Inference

An interpretation derived from evidence.

Example:

> The learner may have strong spatial reasoning.

## Hypothesis

A provisional explanation or possibility that can be tested.

Example:

> Industrial design may be a promising career direction.

## Career hypothesis

A provisional claim that a career, career family, or work environment may be worth further exploration.

## Learner model

A revisable representation of what the system currently understands about the learner.

## Interest

Sustained or situational attraction to a topic, activity, or domain.

Interest is not identical to ability.

## Aptitude

Potential to develop competence in a particular area.

The project should use this term carefully because aptitude is difficult to infer reliably.

## Demonstrated ability

Capability supported by observed performance.

## Learning velocity

The rate or pattern at which competence changes through learning and feedback.

## Career fit

A provisional relationship between a person and a form of work, considering more than skill alone.

Possible dimensions include:

* ability;
* interest;
* motivation;
* environment;
* values;
* constraints;
* lifestyle;
* opportunity.

## Provenance

Information about where evidence came from and under what conditions it was produced.

## Uncertainty

The degree to which a conclusion remains unresolved or weakly supported.

## Missing evidence

Evidence that would be useful for evaluating a hypothesis but has not yet been collected.

Missing evidence is not negative evidence.

## Career experiment

An activity intentionally designed to help a learner gather evidence about a possible career direction.

## Human-in-the-loop

A system design in which meaningful human judgment remains part of the reasoning or decision process.

## Evidence gap

A meaningful area of uncertainty that could potentially be reduced through further observation or experimentation.
