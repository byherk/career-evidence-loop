# Literature Notes

This directory contains working notes on papers, books, reports, frameworks, and other research relevant to Career Evidence Loop.

These notes are not intended to replace the original sources.

Each note should ideally distinguish between:

## What the source says

Claims, findings, methods, or concepts supported directly by the source.

## Interpretation

How the contributor understands the source.

## Relevance to Career Evidence Loop

Why the source may matter to the project.

## Limitations

Important caveats, methodological limitations, or reasons for caution.

## Open questions

Questions raised by the source that may deserve further investigation.

Where possible, include the full citation and DOI or stable URL.
