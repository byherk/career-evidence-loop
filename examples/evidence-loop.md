# Example Evidence Loop

## Step 1 — Interest signal

Learner repeatedly explores marine science.

Current conclusion:

Sustained interest signal.

Not:

Marine scientist aptitude.

## Step 2 — Learning task

Learner completes introductory marine ecology activities.

Evidence:

* strong conceptual understanding;
* high persistence;
* good data interpretation.

## Step 3 — Initial career hypothesis

Marine science may be worth exploring further.

## Step 4 — Missing evidence

Unknown:

* fieldwork tolerance;
* comfort on boats;
* laboratory work;
* long-term scientific data collection;
* mathematical demands.

## Step 5 — Career experiment

Participate in a supervised marine-science field experience or realistic simulation.

## Step 6 — Expert evaluation

A marine educator records structured observations.

## Step 7 — Hypothesis update

Possible outcomes:

### Stronger

The learner performs well and enjoys fieldwork.

### Weaker

The learner enjoys the science but strongly dislikes field conditions.

### Redirected

The learner prefers data analysis and modeling over field research.

A different career hypothesis may become more relevant.

## Lesson

The goal is not to prove that the first recommendation was correct.

The goal is to learn more.
