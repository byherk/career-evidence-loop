# Example Career Hypothesis

Fictional example.

## Career hypothesis

Industrial design may be a promising direction for further exploration.

## Supporting evidence

* sustained interest in physical products;
* strong performance in spatial-reasoning tasks;
* repeated voluntary sketching and prototyping;
* positive instructor feedback on problem decomposition;
* rapid improvement after critique.

## Contradictory or weaker evidence

* limited experience interviewing users;
* inconsistent documentation of design decisions.

## Missing evidence

* material selection;
* fabrication;
* real-user feedback;
* deadline pressure;
* collaborative design work.

## Current confidence

Provisional / moderately supported

The exact confidence framework has not yet been defined.

## Next experiment

Complete a short product-design challenge involving:

1. a real user problem;
2. concept development;
3. prototype creation;
4. user feedback;
5. professional critique.

## Interpretation

The purpose of the experiment is not to confirm the hypothesis.

It is to produce evidence capable of either strengthening or weakening it.
