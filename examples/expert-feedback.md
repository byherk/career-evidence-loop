# Example Expert Feedback

Fictional example.

## Context

A learner completed a supervised cooking exercise.

## Evaluator

Experienced culinary instructor.

## Task

Prepare two dishes under a fixed time constraint.

## Structured observations

### Knife skills

7 / 10

Good basic control. Some inconsistency remains under time pressure.

### Timing

9 / 10

Strong sequencing and awareness of concurrent tasks.

### Hygiene

10 / 10

Consistently maintained throughout the exercise.

### Seasoning

6 / 10

Technically acceptable but inconsistent.

### Response to feedback

9 / 10

Adjusted technique quickly after feedback.

## Qualitative note

The learner remained calm and organized throughout the task and appeared energized rather than discouraged by time pressure.

## Important limitation

This is one evaluator observing one task.

It should not be treated as a complete assessment of professional cooking ability.

## Possible evidence created

* strong timing under pressure;
* strong responsiveness to feedback;
* consistently good hygiene;
* seasoning remains an area requiring further observation.
